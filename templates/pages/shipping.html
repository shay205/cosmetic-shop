How ordering works

  1. Place your order on our website and keep your order number (it looks like GB-000042).
  2. We contact you on the phone number you gave us to confirm your order and delivery details.
  3. We prepare and send your order.
  4. You pay on delivery or by phone, as agreed when we confirm. Online payment isn't available yet.

Tracking your order

If you ordered while logged in, open My orders in your account menu to see your order's status: Pending, Confirmed, Shipped, Delivered or Cancelled. Questions about a delivery? Quote your order number on our Contact page.

Problems with your delivery

If your order arrives damaged or incorrect, please contact us as soon as possible. See our Returns page for what happens next.
