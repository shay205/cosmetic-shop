Ordering

Do I need an account to order?

No. You can check out as a guest. If you create an account and order while logged in, your orders appear under My orders in your account menu. Orders placed as a guest aren't linked to an account.

How do I know my order went through?

After you click Place Order, you'll see a thank-you page with your order number (it looks like GB-000042). Please keep it. We'll then contact you on the phone number you gave us to confirm your order.

Can I change or cancel my order?

Contact us as soon as possible with your order number. We can usually help before the order is shipped. See Returns for more.

Why can't I add more of an item to my cart?

The quantity in your cart can't go above what we have in stock. If an item is sold out, it shows as out of stock and can't be added.
